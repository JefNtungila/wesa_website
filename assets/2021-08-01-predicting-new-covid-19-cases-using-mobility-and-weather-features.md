This distributed data analysis project seeks to predict new cases of COVID-19 in the Greater London Area (GLA) by utilising weather and mobility features. The study focuses on the GLA to minimise variance and noise in weather conditions and mobility variables.
```
 
```
The weather features are derived from public data provided by the National Centers for Environmental Information. Mobility features are sourced from Google Mobility Data and Transport for London transportation data, both publicly accessible. COVID-19 data is sourced from local London authorities.
```
 
```
Whilst the project's emphasis lies in its Machine Learning and High-Performance Computing engineering aspects, its research question carries broader implications for business and organisations. Understanding how mobility and weather conditions influence new COVID-19 cases holds significant current relevance across various spheres including society, politics, and economics.
