As part of a technical challenge, I undertook the analysis of customer Telco telecom churn data from Kaggle.
```
 
```
Churn analysis involves predicting customers who are likely to leave. The definition of "retention" depends on the specific business context and requires alignment with stakeholders. A related analysis, known as retention or survival analysis, focuses on customers most likely to remain (analogous to patients likely to survive).
```
 
```
Churn analysis could involve customers canceling their subscription or discontinuing specific application usage. As with all data science projects, collaboration with stakeholders is essential to ensure that the analysis delivers value to the business.
```
 
```
Churn analysis is essential because customer retention is often more cost-effective than customer acquisition. In saturated markets, where new customer acquisition is limited, every customer lost goes to a competitor. Hence, retaining existing customers gains critical importance.
```
 
```
Churn analysis predicts customers who are on the verge of leaving the ecosystem. Taking preventive measures, such as targeted email campaigns or personalised price-reduction offers, can persuade them to stay, resulting in tangible business value. However, such measures should be agreed upon with business stakeholders.
```
 
```
Churn analysis often involves an imbalanced classification problem for healthy businesses. A majority of customers are usually retained, and only a few churn. This project analysed approximately 7K Telco customers. Exploratory data analysis was performed on their demographic attributes and customer information. About 27% of customers had churned within the last month, whilst 73% were retained, with similar patterns across genders.
```
 
```
The analysis revealed that drivers of churn included variables like total charges over the customer's lifetime, monthly charges, and the presence of online security. Higher customer tenure and overall charges reduced the likelihood of churn. However, high monthly charges in a given month increased the likelihood of churn. Customers without online security were more likely to churn.
```
 
```
A classification model was developed to detect customers likely to churn. To enhance sensitivity to the minority class (the 23% likely to churn), data was synthesised using SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique).
```
 
```
To leverage value from the churn analysis, the next steps would require alignment with the business. Questions like targeting customers most likely to churn, setting probability thresholds, and identifying high-revenue customers for outreach would arise. This might involve a recency-frequency-monetary analysis. True value from Data Science emerges when business and data scientists collaborate.
